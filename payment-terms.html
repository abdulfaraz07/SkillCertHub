Payment Terms & Conditions

Rules for ₹149 certificate access and online payments.

This Payment Terms page explains the rules for paid certificate access and other paid features on SkillCert Hub.

1. Certificate fee

The current certificate access fee displayed on the website is ₹149, unless a different price is clearly shown at checkout. Prices may be changed for future purchases.

2. Payment gateway

Payments are processed through the payment gateway integrated with SkillCert Hub. The gateway may support UPI, cards, net banking and other methods depending on its availability. Payment credentials are entered through the gateway checkout and are subject to the gateway’s security and terms.

3. When certificate access is unlocked

After a successful payment, SkillCert Hub verifies the transaction with the payment gateway before unlocking the certificate. A student must not rely on a screenshot, bank notification or manual “payment completed” confirmation as proof of payment.

4. Failed, pending or cancelled payments

  • If a payment fails, certificate access remains locked.
  • If a payment is pending, wait for the gateway status to be confirmed before attempting another payment.
  • If money is debited but the website still shows an unsuccessful or pending status, contact support with the transaction/order reference. Do not make repeated payments unless instructed.

5. Refunds and cancellations

Refund eligibility depends on the nature of the purchase, whether certificate access has already been unlocked/downloaded, the payment gateway status and applicable law. Any approved refund will normally be processed back through the original payment method. Where a separate refund policy is published, that policy will apply.

6. Duplicate payments

If the same order is paid more than once because of a technical or user error, the duplicate transaction should be reported with the relevant transaction references so it can be reviewed and, where applicable, refunded.

7. Fraud and misuse

We may suspend access, investigate transactions or refuse service where we reasonably suspect fraud, chargeback abuse, unauthorized payment, identity misuse or attempts to bypass the payment system.

8. Payment receipts

Payment confirmations may be provided by the payment gateway and/or SkillCert Hub. Keep the order ID and transaction reference until the certificate is successfully accessed.
