Privacy Policy

How SkillCert Hub handles student and transaction information.

This Privacy Policy explains how SkillCert Hub (“SkillCert”, “we”, “us”) may collect, use and protect information when you use our website, courses, quizzes and certification services.

1. Information we collect

  • Registration information such as full name, email address and mobile number.
  • Account, course, quiz and certificate information, including scores and certificate details.
  • Payment and transaction information needed to process purchases. Card, UPI and other payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment provider; SkillCert does not intentionally store full payment credentials.
  • Technical information such as browser/device information, IP address, logs and basic usage information where required for security and operation.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage student accounts.
  • To send OTPs and important service notifications.
  • To provide courses, quizzes and certificates.
  • To process payments and reconcile transactions.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • To improve the website, courses and user experience.

3. OTP and third-party services

We may use third-party providers such as MSG91 for OTP verification, a payment gateway for payments, and Supabase for application data and backend services. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should protect their passwords, OTPs and devices.

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain certificates and records, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes and prevent fraud.

6. Your choices

You may contact SkillCert Hub through the contact details provided on the website to request correction or deletion of information, subject to legal, security and record-keeping requirements.

7. Policy changes

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
